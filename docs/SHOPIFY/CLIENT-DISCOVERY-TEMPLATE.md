# Client Discovery — AI Chat & Proactive Engagement

**Purpose:** capture how the merchant wants their Automatos bot to behave on their Shopify storefront, before we configure it. This is a template — copy it, rename for the merchant (e.g. `CLIENT-DISCOVERY-INBUILDUK.md`), fill in their answers, and use it as the source of truth when configuring the workspace.

**Architecture context (so questions make sense):**
- Anything about **how the bot looks and when it engages** is configured Shopify-side (theme settings, Automatos dashboard).
- Anything about **what the bot says and how it thinks** is configured Automatos-side (skill, persona, knowledge graph, agent settings).
- Both can be changed later without re-installing the app — these answers are starting points, not commitments.

**How to use this document:**
- Walk through it with the merchant in a 30–45 min conversation. Don't email it cold — half the answers come out of follow-up questions.
- Skip sections that aren't relevant yet. v1 doesn't need every answer.
- Keep merchant answers in plain language. We'll translate to config later.

---

## 1. The business in one paragraph

> Helps us write a persona that sounds like *them*, not generic AI.

- What does your business sell, in your own words?
- Who are your customers — trade pros, consumers, both? Roughly what split?
- What's one thing you're known for that competitors don't do?
- Where do most of your sales come from — repeat customers, search traffic, ads, referrals?

---

## 2. Bot scope — what should it help with?

> Helps us pick which agent skills to enable and which to leave off.

Tick the ones the merchant wants the bot to handle (and add notes on any nuance):

- [ ] **Product questions** — specs, compatibility, "is this right for my project?"
- [ ] **Order tracking** — "where's my order?" lookups via Shopify
- [ ] **Returns / refunds** — explain policy (the bot does NOT process — it explains and escalates)
- [ ] **Stock checks** — "do you have X in stock?"
- [ ] **Recommendations** — "what else works with this?", related products, alternatives
- [ ] **Bulk / trade enquiries** — pricing for quantities, trade account signup
- [ ] **Technical / compliance questions** — certifications, regs, fitting guides
- [ ] **General store info** — opening hours, contact, delivery zones
- [ ] **Other (please specify):** _______

> What should the bot **explicitly never** answer? (e.g. legal advice, medical, financial; anything competitor comparisons; staff/wholesale margins)

---

## 3. Brand voice & personality

> Goes straight into the agent's persona prompt.

- Three adjectives that describe how you talk to customers (e.g. "expert, plain-spoken, helpful — never pushy")
- One brand you sound similar to — and one you definitely don't
- Any pet phrases or words you use a lot? Any words you ban (e.g. "guys", "amazing", over-apologising)?
- Formal / informal? "You" vs "we"?
- Should the bot have a name, or just identify as "the [Brand] assistant"?

---

## 4. Proactive engagement (NEW — the popup feature)

> This is the part that makes the bot context-aware and engaging vs. sitting silently in the corner.

### 4a. Should the bot reach out first, or wait to be opened?

- [ ] Wait silently (default chat icon only) — safest, least intrusive
- [ ] Reach out with a small notification — engages but can feel pushy if mistuned
- [ ] Both — depends on page (e.g. silent on homepage, proactive on product pages)

### 4b. What triggers the popup? (pick one or rank top 2)

- [ ] **Time on page** — e.g. after 20 seconds (works for browsing intent)
- [ ] **Scroll depth** — e.g. when shopper has read past the gallery (works for serious interest)
- [ ] **Exit intent** — when shopper's mouse moves toward the back/close button (last-chance save)
- [ ] **Idle** — no scroll/click for 15 seconds (low-intent rescue)
- [ ] **Cart abandonment** — item added but no checkout after N minutes
- [ ] **Returning visitor** — they've been here before but haven't bought

### 4c. How often is too often?

- Once per product page per session?
- Once per visit (across whole site)?
- Once per day?
- Other: _______

### 4d. What does the popup say?

- A canned line ("Need a hand picking the right panel?") — fast, generic
- A contextual line generated by the bot based on the product they're viewing — slower (~1–2s) but feels personal
- Pick by page type — canned on collection pages, contextual on product pages

### 4e. What does it look like?

- Floating bubble in corner (chat-style)
- Card slide-in from edge of screen (more visible)
- Centre-screen modal — most prominent but most intrusive (we'd recommend against)
- Other: _______

### 4f. What if the shopper dismisses it?

- Don't show again that session
- Don't show again that day
- Don't show again unless they navigate to a different product
- Other: _______

## 5. Product recommendations

> "You might also like…" but smarter.

- When the bot suggests products, where should the suggestions come from?
  - [ ] Same category / product type as what they're looking at
  - [ ] Same brand / vendor
  - [ ] Bundle / "frequently bought with" — needs historical order data
  - [ ] Manually curated rules ("if X is viewed, suggest Y and Z")
  - [ ] AI judgement based on store catalog
- How many products at once? (1, 2, 3, more)
- Should the bot show prices? Stock status? Just titles + image?
- Anything that should NEVER be recommended? (e.g. discontinued, out-of-stock, certain brands)

---

## 6. Knowledge sources

> Where does the bot pull facts from when answering?

- Where do your store policies, FAQs, shipping info, returns terms live today? (Shopify pages, PDFs, Notion, Google Docs, internal wiki)
- Do you have product data sheets, spec PDFs, or installation guides we should load?
- Brand voice guide / tone document — does one exist, or should we build it from samples?
- Anything you do NOT want the bot to learn from? (e.g. internal wiki with margin info, supplier pricing)
- How often does this content change? (We need to know how often to refresh the knowledge base)

---

## 7. Escalation to humans

> When does the bot stop trying and hand off?

- Should the bot try to escalate to a human, or just say "contact us" with a link?
- If escalation: where does it go? Email, live chat (e.g. Gorgias / Intercom), Slack, phone callback request, support ticket?
- Are humans available 24/7 or business hours only? What does the bot say outside hours?
- Specific phrases / situations that should ALWAYS escalate (e.g. "speak to a human", anything mentioning a complaint, anything mentioning a return amount above £X)?

---

## 8. Privacy, compliance, consent

- Are you in the UK / EU? (Affects GDPR cookie handling)
- Should the bot only load AFTER the shopper accepts marketing cookies?
- Anything you can't store about shoppers? (We default to storing conversation transcripts for the merchant to review — confirm OK)
- Any regulated language? (e.g. fire safety claims, medical claims, financial advice)
- Customers under 18 — possible audience? Affects content filtering.

---

## 9. Visual design

> Cosmetics. We'll match brand and theme.

- Primary brand colour (hex code)
- Where on the page should the chat sit? Bottom-right (default), bottom-left, top-right, top-left
- Light theme, dark theme, or auto-match the storefront theme?
- Any logo or icon for the bot avatar?
- Should the bot have a typing indicator / "typing…" animation? (Looks more human; some merchants find it cheesy)

---

## 10. Rollout & approval

- Which theme is the bot enabled in for testing? (e.g. "AI Testing", duplicated theme)
- Who needs to approve before going live on the published theme?
- Are we measuring anything during the test period? (Conversion lift, chat volume, dismiss rate, escalation rate)
- Target go-live date?
- Who's the day-to-day point of contact for tweaks during the trial?

---

## 11. Success criteria

> So everyone agrees what "good" looks like before we ship.

- What does success in 30 days look like for you? (e.g. "X conversations / week", "Y% of pre-sale enquiries answered without human", "Z fewer 'where's my order?' emails")
- What would tell you to turn it off? (Worst-case acceptable behaviour)
- Anything specific you want to A/B test against?

---

## 12. Things we'll handle without bothering you

> So the merchant knows what's automatic vs. what they're signing off on.

- Bot persona drafted from sections 1–3, sent to you for sign-off before going live.
- Knowledge base built from your existing pages + anything you upload — we'll show you what was indexed.
- Tone calibration during the first week based on real conversation reviews.
- Per-page proactive triggers calibrated based on traffic data after a few days live.
- Monthly review of conversations + suggested skill upgrades.

## Appendix — for the implementer (not the merchant)

After the merchant fills this in:

1. Copy answers from §1 + §3 into a draft persona, paste into the agent's `custom_persona_prompt`.
2. Section §2 → enable/disable agent skills in their workspace.
3. Section §4 → translates to widget config in the Automatos dashboard (proactive triggers, frequency caps, popup style).
4. Section §5 → recommendation source config (likely Composio or RAG, depending on what the merchant has).
5. Section §6 → knowledge ingestion plan (which docs, which connectors).
6. Section §7 → escalation handler config + after-hours messaging.
7. Section §8 → consent integration in the Liquid block (`respect_consent` setting).
8. Section §9 → theme block settings (color, position, theme).
9. Section §10–11 → success-criteria doc you review against in week 4.

If the merchant skipped a section, set sane defaults and flag in their `docs/SHOPIFY/CLIENTS.md` notes column for follow-up.
